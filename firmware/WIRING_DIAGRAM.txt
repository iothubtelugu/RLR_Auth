3. R307 / AS608 OPTICAL SENSOR 6-PIN JST CONNECTOR WIRE COLORS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most standard R307 / AS608 optical fingerprint modules come with a 6-wire ribbon cable:

  Pin 1: RED    -> VCC (Connect to ESP32 VIN or External 5V)
  Pin 2: BLACK  -> GND (Connect to ESP32 GND)
  Pin 3: YELLOW -> TXD (Connect to ESP32 GPIO 16 - RX2)
  Pin 4: GREEN  -> RXD (Connect to ESP32 GPIO 17 - TX2)
  Pin 5: BLUE   -> WAKEUP / TOUCH (Connect to GPIO 4 or leave disconnected)
  Pin 6: WHITE  -> 3.3V OUT (Do NOT connect)

*(Note: On some factory batches, TXD is Green and RXD is White. If the ESP32 says
 "Did not find fingerprint sensor", swap GPIO 16 and GPIO 17 connections)*


4. POWER SUPPLY RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Optical fingerprint sensors draw ~80mA to 120mA when the optical prism LED lights up.
- ESP32 Wi-Fi bursts draw up to 250mA-350mA during radio transmissions.
- RECOMMENDATION: Power the system using a high-quality 5V 2A USB wall adapter connected
  to the ESP32 Micro-USB / USB-C port, or wire an external regulated 5V 2A power supply
  directly to the VIN and GND pins.
- Avoid powering the optical sensor from the ESP32 3.3V pin, as 3.3V often lacks sufficient
  optical brightness, leading to blurry scans or "Sensor not found" errors.


5. AUDIO-VISUAL SIGNALING BEHAVIOR
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTION / EVENT             BLUE LED       RED LED        BUZZER SOUND
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Boot / Connecting Wi-Fi    Blinking (2Hz) OFF            Silent
Connected & Ready          Double Flash   OFF            Ascending Welcome Chime
Finger Placed on Scanner   Solid ON       OFF            1 Quick Chirp (30ms)
ENTRY Recorded (Success)   Solid ON (2s)  OFF            Double High Beep (2.2kHz + 2.8kHz)
EXIT Recorded (Success)    Solid ON (2s)  OFF            Triple High Beep (2kHz + 2.4kHz)
Access Denied / Not Reg.   OFF            3 Blinks       3 Error Beeps (800Hz)
Cooldown Active (< 15s)    OFF            2 Blinks       2 Short Warning Buzzes (1.2kHz)
Wi-Fi / Network Error      OFF            Slow Blink     Long Low Tone (600ms)
Enrollment Mode Active     Fast Flash     OFF            Continuous Prompt Beeps
